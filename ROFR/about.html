About Giribhumi

The Back ground for enacting ROFR Act is
  • Traditional Rights of Tribal community’s Example: ancestral land collection of minor forest production, grazing or cultivation were not recognised.
  • Tribes displaced due to dams, mines and other mega projects were often settled in forest land without legal process.
  • As result of traditional right holders on forest land were termed as “encroachers”.
  • To address longstanding insecurity of tenurial rights and access to resources of ST’s & OTFD’s, Parliament enacted FRA 2006, which was notified on 31-12-2007.
Salient features of ROFR Act
  • To recognise and record rights of Forest Dwellers who have been residing in such forests for generations.
  • Empower the forest dwellers with the power to protect and manage.
  • Individual Rights relate to Habitation & Cultivation for livelihood.
  • Community Rights relate to Collection of minor forest produce (MFP), fishing (using water bodies), grazing & Community Forest Resource (Protection, Conservation & Management for sustainable use).

    The channels of process of Forest Rights claims are Gram Sabha to Forest Rights Committee (FRC) to Sub Division Level Committee (SDLC) to District Level Committee (DLC) and State Level Monitoring Committee (SLMC).
Eligibility
  • The Act is applicable for those Scheduled Tribes and Other Traditional Forest Dwellers who were in occupation of the forest land for their habitation or livelihood, prior to 13-12-2005.
  • Individual claimants (for family), are eligible for the land under occupation by them, or a maximum of 4 hectares of land whichever is less.
  • The forest land to be diverted in the case of community claims is less than 1 hectare in each case but should not exceed felling of 75 trees per hectare.
Forest Rights Committee (FRC):

Composition:

  • Election of members from Grama Sabha (GS) first meeting.
  • Minimum ten members not exceeding 15 members.
  • 2/3 rd members shall be from Scheduled Tribes
  • 1/3rd of such members shall be women
  • FRC will decide Chairperson and Secretary and intimate to SDLC

FRC Functions:

  • Field verification by three Departments (Forest, Revenue and Tribal Welfare) of the claimants’ lands.
  • Mapping of the claimant’s lands and forwarding to Grama Sabha (GS).
